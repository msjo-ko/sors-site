Privacy Policy

Sors Vitae: Life Lottery (인생뽑기) · 건강한고양이 (Geonganhan Goyangi), developer name Sors
Effective: October 15, 2026 · 한국어

건강한고양이 ("the developer") makes and distributes Sors Vitae: Life Lottery ("the app"). The app has no sign-up, no login and no developer server; it is an offline game, so the developer does not collect or store your personal data. Ads and purchases in the app are provided through Google services, and Google processes some information when they are used. This policy explains how.

1. Information the developer collects

None. Your game progress (current life, collection, achievements, past lives, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the device.

If you email us, your email address and message stay in the developer's mailbox. When you use the app's "Report a problem" button, the email body also includes the app version, device and browser information (User-Agent), screen size, number of saved records and the last error messages (you can read and delete these before sending). We use them only to answer you and fix the problem, and delete them within one year after the conversation ends.

2. Advertising

The app shows no ads until you have been born in 10 different countries. After that, a full-screen ad may play when a new life starts. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and Google processes the following information.

ItemDetails
Who processes itGoogle LLC and its affiliates (Google AdMob)
InformationAdvertising ID (Android advertising identifier), device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad impressions and clicks

RetentionAccording to Google's data retention policy. The developer does not receive or keep this information.

Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some other regions, the app shows Google's consent message before ads, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → "🔏 Ad privacy choices" in the app.

3. Controlling ads

  • In Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices "Settings → Privacy → Ads") you can reset or delete your advertising ID. A deleted ID is not used for personalised ads.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in your Google account's Ad settings.
  • If you buy "Remove ads" (one-time purchase), the app stops requesting ads, so the information above is no longer collected through ads.

4. Purchases

Payment and purchase restore for "Remove ads" are handled by Google Play Billing. Card and account details are never passed to the developer; the app only asks Google whether your Google account owns the product. Google's handling of payment information follows the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google backup (Android)

If Google backup is turned on for your device, your game progress may be stored in your own Google account backup and restored on a new device. The developer cannot access this backup.

6. Backup · New phone

Files or codes made with the app's "Backup · New phone" feature are saved or sent by you; they never pass through a developer server. They contain only game records and no personal data.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

Because the developer does not keep your personal data, there is no data held by the developer to access, correct, delete or restrict. Game records on your device are deleted when you uninstall the app. For advertising data, you can exercise your rights (including those under the GDPR, UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act) through the Google settings in sections 2 and 3 and the Google Privacy Policy. For any other question, email the contact below.

9. Security

Apart from internet access, the app requests no permissions (contacts, location, storage, camera, etc.). The developer runs no server that stores personal data.
